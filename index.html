Profit formula

Lexcel starts with a standard, accurate formula. Turn on only what applies to your shop.

Business details

The standard formula default, ON

Applied to every sale, always:
Profit per sale = Quantity × (Sell Price − Cost Price) − Discount
Total profit for a period = sum of every sale's profit, minus anything you switch on below.

Optional deductions

Switch these on if they apply to your shop — off by default to keep things simple.
Packaging / container cost
Subtract a per-item packaging cost (cups, bags, boxes) from profit. Set the cost on each product.
Tax on sales
Subtract a percentage of revenue for tax before counting it as profit.
%
Other operating expenses
A fixed amount (rent share, ice, gas, spoilage) subtracted once from the period's total profit — not per sale.
₱

Advanced: write your own formula optional

For confident users only. Leave blank to keep the standard formula above. Available variables per sale: quantity, sellPrice, costPrice, discount, packaging, revenue (= quantity×sellPrice − discount).
